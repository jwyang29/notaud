about
silhouette

Beyond a simple toy, AUD:silhouette is a modern interactive object that breathes inspiration into your space.

Measuring just 60x60x60mm, this compact cube houses advanced photo interrupters and weight sensors that precisely track the steel marble's location. Rely purely on your senses—distinct haptic vibrations will guide you, letting you feel the difference between the right path and a dead end.

how to
play

1. Assemble

Choose your desired inner module and slide it seamlessly into the main outer case.

2. Drop

Insert the steel marble into the starting point to activate the internal sensors.

3. Feel

Follow the haptic feedback—a smooth vibration means right, a sharp pulse warns of a dead end.
