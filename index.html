About

Beyond a simple toy, AUD:silhouette is a modern interactive object that breathes inspiration into your space.
Measuring just 60x60x60mm, this compact cube houses advanced photo interrupters and weight sensors that precisely track the steel marble's location.

Rely purely on your senses—distinct haptic vibrations will guide you, letting you feel the difference between the right path and a dead end.

Interchangeable Modules

Swap the inner modules to experience entirely different mechanics and haptic sensations.

Module 01: The Labyrinth

Navigate the classic maze. Tilt the cube and rely on haptic cues to find the hidden exit through intricate corridors.

Module 02: The Slider

Carve your own path. Slide the internal walls to open new routes and dynamically guide the marble through the shifting structure.

Module 03: The Gear

Timing is everything. Feel the mechanical rhythm and carefully pass the marble through a series of interlocking, rotating gears.

How to Play

1. Assemble

Choose your desired inner module and slide it seamlessly into the main outer case.

2. Drop

Insert the steel marble into the starting point to activate the internal sensors.

3. Feel

Tilt, slide, or rotate. Follow the haptic feedback—a smooth vibration means you are on the right track, while a sharp pulse warns of a dead end.
